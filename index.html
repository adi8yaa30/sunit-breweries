Our Facility

The Sunit Brewery
Process

Every batch runs the same path. German machinery at each stage, FSSC-certified checkpoints between them, and a documented record from grain intake to dispatch.

  1. 01

    Brewhouse

    Malt is milled, mashed and boiled on KHS-specified equipment. Grain bill and temperature curve logged per batch.

  2. 02

    Fermentation

    Wort moves to temperature-controlled cellars. Yeast pitched, gravity tracked daily until the batch hits spec.

  3. 03

    Packaging

    Bottling and canning on German lines — 650ml and 500ml, both formats, no line changeover delay.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    Out through the bonded warehouse under excise supervision, with all-India dispatch permissions in place.
